Ringlock

Founder · Missed-call text-back for trade contractors · Built and launched in 2026
LIVE PRODUCT

When a contractor can't answer, Ringlock texts the caller from the business's number within about 3 seconds, asks for the job address, and forwards the reply to the contractor's cell. Contractors either keep their own number and forward missed calls to Ringlock, or publish a Ringlock tracking number that rings them first with a "press 1 to take it" screen. Each contractor is a separate tenant in Postgres, and every Twilio webhook is signature-verified.

Text-Back Time About 3 seconds
Call Flows Carrier forwarding or tracking number
Lead Capture Asks for the job address
